Privacy, simply

Drafts stay in this tab until you create a link. Selected photos and camera recordings are processed only on this device, never uploaded or sent to an AI provider. Remove clears the app’s in-memory copy; downloaded and externally shared copies remain under your control. Shared messages are encrypted in your browser; the key stays in the link. Encrypted copies expire after 7 days and are deleted on opening. Anyone holding the private link can also delete the message without opening it. We count anonymous actions only, without names, notes, faith choices, message text, video content, analytics cookies, or visitor IDs. A separate secure invitation cookie controls creator access. Invitation and session hashes, credit balances and delivery receipts are stored to enforce credits; no names or emails are required. Short-lived salted network hashes limit abuse; hosting providers still process network traffic. Downloads remain on your device. Counts cannot identify repeat visitors.
